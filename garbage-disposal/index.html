Cabinet flooding from a leaking or jammed disposal

A garbage disposal that leaks into the cabinet or stops with a hum can flood a kitchen quickly, so our garbage disposal repair line in Brigham City, UT stays open 24/7. A garbage disposal is a small grinder under the sink drain that shreds soft food waste so it can wash down the line.

Before anything else, switch the disposal off at the wall and flip its breaker. Never put a hand inside, even when it is off. Set a pan under the leak, run no more water into that sink, and call. Same-day visits are offered when a crew is free.

Callers usually tell us it smells, drains slowly, or sounds like a motor stuck against a wall. All three fit the table. The bottom-of-housing leak is the one people hope to patch, and it almost never holds.

Safe checks before we arrive

  1. Turn the switch off and trip the breaker so the unit cannot start while you look.
  2. Shine a light into the drain opening and remove anything you can see with tongs, never fingers.
  3. Find the hex-shaped socket in the center of the unit's underside, and work a hex key back and forth to free a jam.
  4. Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit once it cools, then restore power and run cold water.
  5. If the unit still hums, or water drips anywhere, stop and call.

Leave off the repeated resets. A disposal that trips again and again has a motor or wiring problem that a button will not solve.

Repair it or put in a new unit

Jams, loose connections and a leaking flange seal are repairs. A rusted housing, a burned-out motor, or a unit that has jammed repeatedly means a replacement. If you bought a disposal already, we install it, and the work sits alongside other plumbing installation jobs that need new connections.

Replacement means removing the old mounting ring, setting the new unit on the sink flange, connecting the drain and dishwasher hose, and wiring or plugging in power. We test it under running water before leaving, with the dishwasher line checked for leaks too.

The sink backs up and the disposal is not the cause

Grease, coffee grounds and starchy scraps pack into the trap and the line behind the disposal. If water rises in the other basin, or the laundry sink gurgles, the blockage sits past the unit. That is a job for drain cleaning, not a new disposal.

Greasy buildup that returns every few months may need a pass with a water jet, since a cable only punches a hole through it. Our hydro jetting crew scours the pipe wall so the opening stays wide.

Dishwasher hose and air gap questions

The dishwasher drains through a short hose into the side of the disposal. If a move or a repair knocked that hose loose, water backs into the cabinet when the dishwasher runs. We reattach it with a proper clamp and make sure the loop is high enough to stop dirty water flowing back.

Some sinks have an air gap, a small cylinder next to the faucet. If it leaks when the dishwasher drains, the hose or the cap is usually the reason, and it is a quick repair. A sink faucet leak that looks like disposal water is covered under faucet repair.

What is not a plumber job

If the outlet is sparking, the cord is hot, or the breaker keeps tripping when the unit is off, call an electrician. A disposal jammed with a utensil is fine for us, but a sink that fills with sewage from the floor drain is a main line problem. Also, a smell of gas in the kitchen means leave first, then call 911 or the gas utility.
