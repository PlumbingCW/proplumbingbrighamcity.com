Plumbing Services in Brigham City, UT

Brigham City Pro Plumbing dispatches licensed plumbers for twenty plumbing services across Brigham City, UT, with a 24/7 emergency line.

Call (855) 575-289024/7 emergency line, any hour.

Twenty services, one phone number

Every service below has its own page that explains the symptoms, how a plumber finds the cause, repair options and the factors that move the price. Pages marked as emergency work are available around the clock. Planned jobs, such as installs, are scheduled during regular hours.

We do not post dollar prices. Each job gets a clear price before work starts, after the plumber sees what is actually wrong.

Not sure which service fits? Describe what you see to a dispatcher at (855) 575-2890. If you want to know where we work, the service area page lists the towns.

Leaks and burst pipes

Leak DetectionFinds hidden leaks behind walls, under floors and in yard lines before repairs begin.Burst Pipe RepairShuts down, repairs and tests split supply lines, including winter freeze damage.RepipingReplaces worn or failing supply lines when patching no longer makes sense.

Drains and sewer

Drain CleaningClears kitchen, tub and branch-line clogs with cable machines.Hydro JettingUses high-pressure water to scour grease, scale and roots from stubborn lines.Sewer Line CleaningClears the main line when sewage backs up into the lowest drain.Sewer Line RepairFixes cracked, offset or root-damaged sewer pipe from house to connection.Sewer Camera InspectionSends a camera through the line to show its true condition, useful before buying a home.

Water heaters

Water Heater RepairDiagnoses leaks, pilot trouble and no-hot-water problems on tank units.Water Heater ReplacementSwaps a failed tank for a new one, sized to the household.Water Heater InstallationPlans venting, expansion control and sizing for new or relocated units.Tankless Water HeaterCovers tankless installs, descaling flushes and error-code repairs.

Fixtures and appliances

Toilet RepairFixes leaks, running tanks, bad seals and stubborn clogs, and sets new toilets.Faucet RepairRepairs drips, worn cartridges and failed supply valves at sinks, tubs and showers.Garbage Disposal RepairRepairs or replaces jammed, leaking or humming disposals.Plumbing InstallationAdds fixtures, appliance hookups and new lines for remodels and additions.

Water quality and groundwater

Water Softener InstallationInstalls conditioners that address the city's very hard water.Sump Pump RepairTests, repairs and replaces pumps that keep basements dry in melt season.Basement WaterproofingHandles the plumbing side of a wet basement, from interior drains to window wells.

Businesses

Commercial PlumbingServes shops, restaurants and offices with after-hours repairs, drains and backflow needs.
