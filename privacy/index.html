Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Brigham City Pro Plumbing handles when you call our line for plumbing service in Brigham City, UT.

What we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details. The way to reach us is by phone at (855) 575-2890. When you call, we ask for information needed to send a plumber, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use it

We use call details to dispatch a licensed plumber to your address, to confirm the visit and to follow up on service. We share the details a plumber needs to do the job. We do not use them for anything unrelated to your request.

Website data

Like most sites, this one may use basic technical logs and analytics to see how pages perform. These do not identify you by name. Your browser settings control cookies.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about your call, or ask us to correct it, by calling (855) 575-2890. Our address is 647 S Main St, Brigham City, UT 84302.

We may update this page from time to time. The current version is always the one on this site.

Questions about your information?

Call (855) 575-2890 and a dispatcher will help.
