How a split pipe is handled

Never use a torch or heat gun to thaw a pipe inside a wall. A hair dryer on low heat, moved along the pipe, is safer, but only after the main is closed. If the pipe is already split, the repair is a cut-out and new section, covered on our burst pipe repair page.

Pipes on exterior walls, in crawl spaces and in garages freeze first, so those are the places to check after a cold snap. Open the cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls to let room air reach the lines.

Spring thaw in the Bear River Valley

Tremonton sits in the Bear River Valley, with the Malad River flowing through the city, and annual precipitation of 16.07 inches. When 40 inches of snow melt over a few warm weeks, ground near foundations stays saturated. We have no record of which Tremonton streets take water, so we will not name any.

What you can do is test the pit, clear window wells and check that downspouts empty away from the wall. A pump that fails during thaw is an emergency, and sump pump repair is one of the calls we expect in March and April.

Summer heat, sprinklers and the water bill

July highs average 91.1 F in Tremonton, so lawns and gardens pull hard on the water system. A broken sprinkler line or a leaking valve can run for weeks before anyone sees a wet patch. A bill that jumps with no change in habits is the usual clue.

Shut the house valve and watch the meter. If the dial still moves, the leak is somewhere between the meter and your fixtures, and leak detection can trace it. Call 811 before any digging, with at least 3 business days of notice.

Before the first hard freeze

  1. Unhook hoses

    Disconnect and drain every hose, then shut the interior valve feeding outdoor spigots.

  2. Wrap exposed pipe

    Insulate lines in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls.

  3. Find your main valve

    Everyone in the house should know where it is and how it turns.

  4. Test the sump pit

    Pour a bucket in and watch the pump clear it before the snow starts.
