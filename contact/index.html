What to have ready before you dial

  • Your street address and a nearby cross street.
  • A short description of what you see or hear.
  • Whether the water is off and where the valve is.
  • A callback number in case the line drops.
  • Photos on your phone, if the damage is visible.

Our address and how to find it

Our address is 647 S Main St, Brigham City, UT 84302, on Brigham City's Main Street. Plumbing work is done at your property, so please call before visiting.

If you are coming into Brigham City on US-89 or US-91 from Box Elder Canyon, or from the I-15 exits, Main Street is the central route. During the weekend after Labor Day, much of Main Street is closed to cars for Peach Days, so plan an alternate approach if you need to reach the address.

Calling about a problem outside your property line

Some issues belong to the city. Residents are not permitted to open city water meters, so a leak on the street side calls for Brigham City Public Works at (435) 734-6615, or the after-hours water line at (435) 723-6868. Permit questions go to the Brigham City building office at (435) 734-6604.

If you smell gas, leave the building first, then call 911 or the gas utility from outside. Do not wait for a plumber.
