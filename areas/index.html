Plumbing Service Areas Around Brigham City, UT

Brigham City Pro Plumbing dispatches licensed plumbers 24/7 across Brigham City, UT and the nearby towns of Perry, Willard and Tremonton.

Call (855) 575-289024/7 emergency line, any hour.

Where dispatch sends crews

Our home base is 647 S Main St in Brigham City, the Box Elder County seat. From there, dispatch covers three neighboring towns that each have their own page: Perry, Willard and Tremonton. Distances below are estimates, so treat them as a guide and let dispatch confirm.

Crews are dispatched across the whole service area, and no town gets a promised arrival time. Distance is only one factor. Call volume and the job itself matter too, and the dispatcher will give you an honest estimate when you call (855) 575-2890.

Perry, UTAbout 4 miles south on US-89, sharing ZIP 84302 with Brigham City, with fruit orchards and fruit stands along the highway.Willard, UTAbout 7 miles south in ZIP 84340, near Willard Bay State Park and the I-15 exit, with pioneer rock homes in town.Tremonton, UTAbout 20 miles north in ZIP 84337 in the Bear River Valley, near the I-15 and I-84 junction.

Other places callers ask about

Callers also ring from Garland (ZIP 84312), which borders Tremonton to the north, and from Honeyville (84314) and Corinne (84307). Some call from Plymouth, and from Mantua, which sits up US-89 and US-91 in Box Elder Canyon.

These places do not have their own pages. If you live in one, call and give the address. The dispatcher will say whether a crew can reach you and what to expect.

Before you call from any town

  • Shut off the main valve if water is moving.
  • Note the street address and the nearest cross street.
  • If the problem is on the street side of the meter, call your water utility.
  • Photograph visible damage before cleaning up.
  • For services, see the full list of plumbing services.

Brigham City residents are not permitted to open city water meters. Contact Public Works at (435) 734-6615 for the city side of a leak.
