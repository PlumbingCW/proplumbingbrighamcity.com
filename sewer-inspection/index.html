What a sewer camera inspection is, and who needs one

A sewer camera inspection pushes a small waterproof camera down your drain line and records what it sees, so you can look at the pipe instead of guessing. It answers a plain question: is the line sound, or is something wrong in the dirt you cannot see?

Most calls come from a few situations. A drain keeps clogging, a buyer wants proof before closing, a seller wants to disclose what is true, or a contractor needs to know where the line runs before digging. Our licensed plumbers book these as planned visits, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

Buying or selling a house in Brigham City

Do you have the equipment? A plain answer

Callers often ask this first, and they are right to. We use a push camera on a flexible rod with a lit head, a recorder that saves the footage and a locating receiver that picks up a signal from the camera head. That combination lets us see the pipe, save the video and mark the position above ground.

The camera cannot see through standing water or heavy buildup. If the line is partly full, we may clear it first with a cable or hydro jetting, then re-run the camera. We say so before work starts.

Locating the line and marking the spot

  1. Insert the camera

    We enter through a cleanout, a pulled toilet or another access point.

  2. Follow the pipe

    The camera advances while the locator tracks the signal at the surface.

  3. Mark depth and position

    We paint or flag the ground at the problem and note how deep the pipe lies.

  4. Call 811 before digging

    Blue Stakes of Utah asks for a call to 811 at least 3 business days before excavation. Plan any dig around that.

Locating helps even without a problem. Knowing where the line runs keeps a fence post, a new patio or a tree from landing on top of it.

After the video: what happens next

You choose. If the pipe is clean, you are done and you keep the video. If it needs cleaning, we can do sewer line cleaning on the same visit or another day. If the pipe is broken, we outline the repair options in sewer line repair terms and give you the price before work starts.
